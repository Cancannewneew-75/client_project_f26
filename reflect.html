Reflecting on Your Experience

Finish and Reflect on the Project

At the end of the experience, the team should complete a responsible handoff, review the results, and identify what each student learned from the project.

Complete the Experience

Prepare the Handoff

  • Organize the final files and documentation.
  • Explain how the partner can use and update the work.
  • Record unfinished tasks and known limitations.

Evaluate the Project

  • Compare the results with the original goals.
  • Ask the partner for feedback.
  • Identify what worked and what could be improved.

Communicate Your Learning

  • Describe your individual contribution.
  • Connect the experience to your learning goals.
  • Use the project in a resume, portfolio, or interview.
