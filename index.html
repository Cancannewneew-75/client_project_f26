Welcome to the UMSI Engaged Learning Office

Build Skills. Work with Partners. Have an Impact.

The UMSI Engaged Learning Office connects students with opportunities to apply classroom knowledge to real challenges. Use this site to prepare for your experience, begin a strong partnership, complete your work responsibly, and explain what you learned.

Why Use This Resource?

Real-world projects rarely follow a perfect plan. This site brings guidance for each stage of an engaged learning experience into one place, so students can spend less time searching for materials and more time learning, collaborating, and moving their projects forward.

  • Follow a clear process: Move from preparation and project launch through active work, reflection, and responsible closeout.
  • Find practical support: Access templates, checklists, examples, and guidance for common project needs.
  • Build professional skills: Strengthen communication, teamwork, project management, and ethical decision-making.
  • Turn experience into evidence: Explain your learning and impact in resumes, portfolios, and interviews.

  • Study Abroad — Build intercultural awareness and apply your learning in an international setting.

About the Engaged Learning Office

The Engaged Learning Office works with partners across industries and sectors, including technology companies, grassroots nonprofits, and cultural heritage institutions, to provide project-based opportunities for UMSI students.

Its mission is to facilitate transformational learning experiences and enable innovative, sustainable, and mutually beneficial information solutions for local and global communities.
